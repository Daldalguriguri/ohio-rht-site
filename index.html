On the menu

The Restaurant, Hotel and Tourism major is a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) program in the Department of Recreation, Sport Pedagogy, and Consumer Sciences, part of the Patton College of Education.

Course Menu

  • Introduction to Hospitality
  • Hotel & Lodging Operations
  • Restaurant Operations
  • Food and Culture
  • MICE & Event Planning
  • Principles of Tourism
  • Marketing for Hospitality and Tourism
  • Accounting in Hospitality
  • Career Advancement for Hospitality Professionals
  • Hospitality Internship

Commercial kitchen

Run food and beverage operations and catering in a commercial kitchen laboratory.

Demonstration classroom

Practice event management and service in a demonstration classroom.

Out in the field

Gain industry experience through a hospitality internship and study tours, with mentorship from industry professionals.

Where it leads

  • Hotel and restaurant companies
  • Campus and school dining
  • Event planning and catering
  • Tourism organizations

Graduates typically start in entry- or mid-level management roles in places like these. A master's degree can open the way to senior management and executive roles.

How to apply

For first-year students entering in the fall at the Athens campus. Dates and amounts can change each year, so always check the official pages.

  1. Nov 15

    Early Action deadline

    Apply by this date to get priority consideration for first-year scholarships.

  2. Jan 15

    Submit the FAFSA

    Recommended date. Many scholarships also look at financial need.

  3. Feb 1

    Final deadline

    The last day to apply for fall admission.

What you need

  • Application for admission
  • $55 application fee
  • Official high school transcript, sent by your counselor
  • ACT or SAT scores are optional
